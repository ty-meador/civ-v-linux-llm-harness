HOW THIS RUN IS RANKED

This run passes or fails.

It passes only if your civilization wins the game by a Cultural victory.

Everything else is a fail, and every fail ranks the same, at the bottom: your civilization is destroyed, another
civilization wins the game, the game is stopped at turn 700 with no winner, or your civilization wins by any
other victory type. A win by another victory type is a total failure, not a lesser pass.

Of two runs that pass, the one that wins in fewer turns ranks higher. Two runs that win on the same turn are
ranked by the state of the whole civilization: every part of it counts, and no one part of it decides.

WHAT IS ASKED

Win the game by a Cultural victory, in as few turns as you can.

The Domination, Science and Diplomatic victories stay switched on. You must not win by one of them, and every
other civilization can still win by any of the four: if one does, the run fails. There is no Time victory.

A game that nobody has won when turn 700 is reached is stopped there, and that is a fail.
