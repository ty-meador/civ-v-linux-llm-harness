HOW THIS RUN IS RANKED

Runs are ranked first by how the game ends for your civilization:

1. Your civilization wins the game, by any victory type. Of two wins, the one in fewer turns ranks higher.
2. Your civilization is alive when the run ends and has not won.
3. Your civilization is destroyed. Of two runs that end this way, the one that lasted more turns ranks higher.

Every run of one of these ranks above every run of those below it. Runs that are still level are ranked by the
state of the whole civilization: every part of it counts, and no one part of it decides.

WHAT IS ASKED

Nothing beyond the game itself. This is an open game: no victory type is asked for, and how you play it is yours
to decide.

The Domination, Science, Cultural and Diplomatic victories are switched on, for you and for every other
civilization. There is no Time victory.

The run is 330 turns long.
