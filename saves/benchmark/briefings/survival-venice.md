HOW THIS RUN IS RANKED

A run either wins the game or it does not, and the two are ranked apart.

A run that wins, by any victory type, ranks above every run that does not. Of two wins, the one in fewer turns
ranks higher. Two runs that win on the same turn are ranked by the state of the whole civilization: every part
of it counts, and no one part of it decides.

A run that does not win is ranked by the number of turns your civilization survived: the turn it was destroyed
on, or the turn the game ended on with your civilization still alive. More turns rank higher.

WHAT IS ASKED

To win with any available victory type, and if you cannot, to survive as many turns as possible. Your
civilization starts in a place that is very hard to hold.

WARNING

You play Venice. Venice cannot build Settlers. Your neighbours are militarily aggressive and will attack.

The Domination, Science, Cultural and Diplomatic victories are switched on, for you and for every other
civilization. There is no Time victory.

A game that nobody has won when turn 700 is reached is stopped there: a run that ends that way has not won, and
has survived 700 turns.
